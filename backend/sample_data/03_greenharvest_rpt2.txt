Regulation 23 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 - Related Party Transaction

Sub: Disclosure of Related Party Transaction approved by the Audit Committee

This is to inform that the Audit Committee, at its meeting held on 18th July 2026, approved a
related party transaction whereby the Company will lease warehousing facilities from Deshmukh
Logistics and Warehousing LLP, an entity in which Mr. R. Deshmukh, Managing Director of the
Company, and his spouse together hold a controlling interest, for an aggregate lease value not
exceeding Rs. 9.5 crore over a 3-year term commencing 1st August 2026.

The transaction has been assessed as being on an arm's length basis, based on an internal
valuation exercise. No external valuer's report has been appended to this disclosure.

This disclosure is made in compliance with Regulation 23(9) of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.
