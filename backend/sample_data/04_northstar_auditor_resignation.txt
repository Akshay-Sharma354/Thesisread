Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 - Resignation of Statutory Auditor

Sub: Resignation of Statutory Auditor of the Company

We wish to inform you that M/s Kanakia & Associates, Chartered Accountants, Statutory Auditors
of the Company, have vide their letter dated 22nd July 2026, tendered their resignation from the
office of Statutory Auditor with immediate effect, citing "pre-occupation with other professional
commitments" as the reason.

The resignation comes approximately 14 months into their 5-year term, which commenced at the
Annual General Meeting held in May 2025.

The Audit Committee has taken note of the resignation and the Board will consider the appointment
of a new Statutory Auditor to fill the casual vacancy in due course, subject to shareholder
approval at the next General Meeting.

The Company confirms there is no other material information/reason for the resignation other
than the reason stated above.

For Northstar Pharmaceuticals Limited
Company Secretary
