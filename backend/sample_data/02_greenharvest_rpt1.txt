Regulation 23 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 - Related Party Transaction

Sub: Disclosure of Related Party Transaction approved by the Audit Committee

This is to inform that the Audit Committee, at its meeting held on 10th February 2026, approved
a related party transaction whereby the Company will procure raw material (organic fertilizer
inputs) from Harvest Agri Inputs Private Limited, a company in which Mr. R. Deshmukh, Managing
Director of the Company, holds a 62% equity stake, for an aggregate value not exceeding Rs. 18
crore for FY 2026-27.

The transaction has been assessed as being on an arm's length basis and in the ordinary course
of business, based on comparable market quotations obtained from two independent vendors.

This disclosure is made in compliance with Regulation 23(9) of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

For GreenHarvest Agro Limited
